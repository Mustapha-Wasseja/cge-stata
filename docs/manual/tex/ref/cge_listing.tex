\cgeentry{cge_listing}{Equation listing, infeasibility report and solution listing}

\cgesec{Description}

\cmd{cge_listing} (from version 0.9.5) lists the equations of the model at a
point, as the equation listing and the infeasibility report of a GAMS run do.
For every block of equations of chapter~\ref{chap:equations}, and for the user
equations, it shows the elements in the system, the largest scaled residual and
where it occurs, and the number of elements that are infeasible there, whose
scaled residual exceeds the tolerance. It then lists the infeasible elements,
largest first, with their left and right sides, and, on request, every element
of the blocks named. The point is a stored simulation, the base, or the
current point from which the next solve starts. From version 0.9.8, with
\opt{sol}{ution}, it gives the solution listing that GAMS writes after a solve:
the lower bound, level, upper bound and marginal of every element of the
blocks of variables and equations asked for, with the constraints and their
marginals.

\cgesec{Quick start}

The equations at the last simulation stored
\begin{stcode}
cge_listing
\end{stcode}
The equations that a shock moves, before the model is solved, with every
element of the import prices and of government revenue
\begin{stcode}
cge_shock tm, value(0)
cge_listing, current blocks(P1 I8)
\end{stcode}
Every element of every equation at the simulation \cmd{notariff}, as a dataset
\begin{stcode}
cge_listing, sim(notariff) clear
\end{stcode}
The solution listing of the imports and their premiums, and of import demand
\begin{stcode}
cge_listing, sim(notariff) solution variables(QM RM) blocks(T5)
\end{stcode}

\cgesec{Syntax}

\begin{stsyntax}
\cmdb{cge_listing} [\texttt{,} \argu{options}]\\
\cmdb{cge_listing} \texttt{,} \opt{sol}{ution} [\argu{options}]
\end{stsyntax}

\begin{opttable}
\optgroup{Point}
\optarg{}{sim}{name} & a stored simulation; default the last one stored\\
\opt{}{base} & the base\\
\opt{cur}{rent} & the current point: base values with the current shocks and fixed values\\
\optgroup{Listing}
\optarg{block}{s}{list} & list every element of these blocks\\
\opt{}{all} & list every element of every block\\
\optarg{}{top}{\#} & number of infeasible elements listed; default 20\\
\optarg{tol}{erance}{\#} & scaled residual above which an element is infeasible; default \texttt{1e-8}\\
\opt{}{clear} & replace the data in memory with every element\\
\optgroup{Solution listing}
\opt{sol}{ution} & the solution listing, with bounds and marginals\\
\optarg{var}{iables}{list} & list every element of these variables\\
\end{opttable}

\cgesec{Options}

\begin{optdesc}
\item[\optarg{}{sim}{name}, \opt{}{base}, \opt{cur}{rent}] choose the point;
at most one may be given. The default is the last simulation stored, or the
base if none is. The current point is the base with the shocks and fixed
values that the next \manref{cge_solve} applies, from which it starts: its
listing shows the equations that the shocks move away from the base.

\item[\optarg{block}{s}{list}] lists every element of the blocks named, such as
\cmd{blocks(P1 I8)}, with its left and right sides and its scaled residual.
\opt{}{all} lists every block.

\item[\optarg{}{top}{\#}] sets how many infeasible elements are listed, largest
first; the default is 20.

\item[\optarg{tol}{erance}{\#}] sets the scaled residual above which an element
is infeasible; the default, $10^{-8}$, is the tolerance of the solution check
of \manref{cge_check}.

\item[\opt{}{clear}] replaces the data in memory with one observation per
element of the model's equations, with the variables \cmd{block},
\cmd{element}, \cmd{description}, \cmd{lhs}, \cmd{rhs}, \cmd{residual} (the
left less the right side), \cmd{scaled}, and the indicators \cmd{insystem},
\cmd{replaced} (by a user equation) and \cmd{infeasible}. With
\opt{sol}{ution}, one observation per variable element and per equation
element in the system, with \cmd{kind} (\cmd{VAR} or \cmd{EQU}), \cmd{name},
\cmd{element}, \cmd{lower}, \cmd{level}, \cmd{upper} and \cmd{marginal}; an
infinite bound and a marginal that does not exist are missing.

\item[\opt{sol}{ution}] (from version 0.9.8) gives the solution listing.
\optarg{block}{s}{list} then names the blocks of equations and
\optarg{var}{iables}{list} the variables to list, and \opt{}{all} lists every
block of both. Without them, the listing gives the variables with attributes of
\manref{cge_bound}. The constraints are always listed.
\end{optdesc}

\cgesubsec{Example 5: the solution listing}

From version 0.9.8, the solution listing shows each element as GAMS does
after a solve. A variable fixed under the closure, such as the premium
\cmd{RM} where no constraint frees it, has its level as both bounds; a free one
has the bounds of \manref{cge_bound}, or none. An equation is written with
every term on the left, so its bounds are zero and its level is its residual.
The square system has no marginals, as GAMS's CNS models have none. With a
quota on imports of manufactures, the constraint is listed as block
\cmd{B1}: its level, the room within the bound, is zero since it binds, and
its marginal is the premium that clears the market, the move of
\cmd{RM(com\_man)} from zero:
\stlog{list_solution}
A limit that does not bind has room within its bound and a zero marginal. These
are the slack and the marginal that GAMS reports for the pair in its MCP model,
where the marginal of a row is the level of its column; \manref{cge_gams},
\opt{}{compare}, checks them against GAMS's.

\cgesec{Stored results}

\cmd{cge_listing} stores the following in \cmd{r()}:
\begin{storedres}
\resgroup{Scalars}
\res{r(N_equations)} & number of elements in the system\\
\res{r(N_replaced)} & number of elements replaced by user equations\\
\res{r(N_infeasible)} & number of elements in the system above the tolerance\\
\res{r(N_listed)} & number of elements listed by \cmd{blocks()} or \cmd{all}\\
\res{r(max_residual)} & the largest scaled residual in the system\\
\res{r(tolerance)} & the tolerance\\
\resgroup{Macros}
\res{r(block)} & the block of the largest scaled residual\\
\res{r(element)} & its element\\
\res{r(point)} & the point listed\\
\end{storedres}
With \opt{sol}{ution}, it stores:
\begin{storedres}
\resgroup{Scalars}
\res{r(N_variables)} & number of variable elements\\
\res{r(N_free)} & number of free variable elements\\
\res{r(N_equations)} & number of equation elements in the system\\
\res{r(N_constraints)} & number of constraints\\
\res{r(N_binding)} & number of constraints that bind\\
\res{r(N_atbound)} & number of free elements at a bound of \manref{cge_bound}\\
\resgroup{Macros}
\res{r(point)} & the point listed\\
\resgroup{Matrices}
\res{r(room)} & the room within each constraint's bound\\
\res{r(marginals)} & the marginal of each constraint\\
\end{storedres}

\cgesec{Methods and formulas}

At a point $(x, \theta)$, each element $e$ of block $B$ has the left and right
sides $\ell_{B,e}(x, \theta)$ and $r_{B,e}(x, \theta)$ of chapter~\ref{chap:equations},
the residual $\ell_{B,e} - r_{B,e}$, and the scaled residual
\begin{equation*}
\frac{\ell_{B,e}(x, \theta) - r_{B,e}(x, \theta)}{\sigma_{B,e}}, \qquad
\sigma_{B,e} = \max\bigl(|\ell_{B,e}(x^0, \theta^0)|, |r_{B,e}(x^0, \theta^0)|\bigr),
\end{equation*}
or $\sigma_{B,e} = 1$ when that is at most $10^{-9}$: the residual that
\manref{cge_solve} brings below its tolerance. An element is infeasible when
it is in the system and its scaled residual exceeds the tolerance in absolute
value. Elements outside the model, such as the import price of a commodity
that is not imported, are not listed; elements replaced by user equations are
listed and counted apart.

In the solution listing (from version 0.9.8), the lower and upper bounds of a
variable element are its level if the closure fixes it, and otherwise the
bounds of \manref{cge_bound}, $-\infty$ and $+\infty$ by default. An equation
element is $\ell_{B,e} - r_{B,e} = 0$: its bounds are 0 and its level the
residual. A constraint on $Y$ with bound $b$, type $\sigma$ and adjusting
variable $X$, fixed at $\bar X$ and moving in the direction $\delta$
(chapter~\ref{chap:constraints}), has the level and marginal
\begin{equation*}
g = \sigma\,(b - Y) \ge 0, \qquad h = \delta\,(X - \bar X) \ge 0, \qquad g\,h = 0,
\end{equation*}
the room within the bound and the move of the adjusting variable, in the
units of $Y$ and $X$. In the GAMS program the pair is $\text{BND} \perp \text{SLK}$,
with $\text{BND} = g / s_Y$ and $\text{SLK} = h / s_X$; GAMS gives the
marginal of a row of an MCP model as the level of its column, so that the
marginal of BND times $s_X$ is $h$ and its slack times $s_Y$ is $g$.
