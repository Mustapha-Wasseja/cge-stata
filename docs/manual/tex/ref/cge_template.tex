\cgeentry{cge_template}{Write a template that maps SAM accounts to roles and groups}

\cgesec{Description}

\cmd{cge_template} writes a template with one row per account of the SAM of
the current model, as read, aggregated or balanced. The template is filled in,
in a spreadsheet or any editor, and then used by \manref{cge_map}
\cmd{using} to map the SAM to the model's roles and by
\manref{cge_aggregate} \cmd{using} to merge accounts into groups. A template is
easier to check and to keep than a long \cmd{cge_map} command, and it has no
limit on the number of accounts.

If the model is mapped, the roles and pairs of the template are filled in from
the mapping. The data in memory are not changed.

\cgesec{Quick start}

Write a template of the SAM read by \cmd{cge_sam} to an Excel file
\begin{stcode}
cge_template using sam_roles.xlsx
\end{stcode}
As above, to a CSV file, replacing the file if it exists
\begin{stcode}
cge_template using sam_roles.csv, replace
\end{stcode}
Aggregate and map the SAM with the template, once it has been filled in
\begin{stcode}
cge_aggregate using sam_roles.xlsx
cge_map using sam_roles.xlsx
\end{stcode}

\cgesec{Syntax}

\begin{stsyntax}
\cmdb{cge_template} \cmd{using} \argu{filename} [\texttt{,} \opt{}{replace}]
\end{stsyntax}

\argu{filename} ends in \cmd{.xlsx}, \cmd{.xls} or \cmd{.csv}.

\cgesec{Options}

\begin{optdesc}
\item[\opt{}{replace}] allows an existing file to be replaced.
\end{optdesc}

\cgesec{Remarks and examples}

The template has the columns
\begin{cgetable}{>{\ttfamily}lp{0.72\linewidth}}
\toprule
\normalfont Column & Content\\
\midrule\endhead
\bottomrule\endlastfoot
account & the account label\\
role & the \cmd{cge_map} options that list the account, separated by spaces\\
pair & for an activity, the commodity it is paired with; for a tax on the use of a factor, the factor it taxes\\
group & for aggregation, the account it is merged into\\
row\_total & the account's row total (receipts)\\
column\_total & the account's column total (payments)\\
difference & the row total less the column total\\
\end{cgetable}
An Excel template has the columns in the sheet \cmd{accounts}, and a second
sheet, \cmd{roles}, with notes on the entries of the \cmd{role}, \cmd{pair}
and \cmd{group} columns. Only the columns
\cmd{account}, \cmd{role}, \cmd{pair} and \cmd{group} are read back; the
totals are there to help assign the roles.

\cgesubsec{Roles}

A role is the name of a \manref{cge_map} option:
\begin{cgetable}{>{\ttfamily}lp{0.68\linewidth}}
\toprule
\normalfont Role & Account\\
\midrule\endhead
\bottomrule\endlastfoot
activities & an activity (production sector)\\
commodities & a commodity\\
factors & a factor of production\\
households & a household group, or a non-profit institution serving households\\
enterprises & an enterprise\\
government & the government (one account)\\
savinv & savings--investment (one account)\\
row & the rest of the world (one account)\\
dirtax & the direct tax account, or the government account if direct taxes are paid to it\\
saltax & the sales tax account, or the government account\\
imptax & the import tariff account, or the government account\\
exptax & the export tax account, or the government account\\
acttax & the activity tax account, or the government account\\
vattax & the value added tax account\\
margins & an activity whose margins are netted out of its output (also an activity)\\
territorial & a territorial correction: its commodity and, if there is one, its activity\\
marginaccounts & a margin account paid per unit of composite supply\\
domesticmargins & a margin account paid per unit of domestic sales\\
importmargins & a margin account paid per unit of imports\\
exportmargins & a margin account paid per unit of exports\\
stocks & a stock-change account, merged into savings--investment\\
insurance & the social health insurance fund (one account)\\
factortax & an account of taxes on the use of a factor, named in the pair column\\
factorincometax & the account of taxes on factor income (one account)\\
\end{cgetable}
Roles may be typed in either case, and the singular forms \cmd{activity},
\cmd{commodity}, \cmd{factor}, \cmd{household}, \cmd{enterprise},
\cmd{margin}, \cmd{marginaccount}, \cmd{domesticmargin},
\cmd{importmargin}, \cmd{exportmargin}, \cmd{stock} and \cmd{vat} are also
accepted. An account may have several roles, separated by spaces or commas, where
\cmd{cge_map} allows it: \cmd{activities margins} for a margin activity, or
\cmd{government saltax} when sales taxes are paid to the government account.
A role that is not in the list is an error. Leave the role blank only for an
account without flows, which \cmd{cge_map} accepts unmapped. A share of the
sales tax that is VAT is not a role: it is given to \cmd{cge_map using} with
its option \cmd{vatshare()}.

\cgesubsec{Pairs}

Each activity is paired with a commodity, its primary product. A blank
\cmd{pair} pairs an activity with the commodity whose column pays it most. Give
the pair when the largest sale is not the activity's main product, when two
activities would be paired with the same commodity, or when an activity sells
equal amounts to several commodities; the last two are errors otherwise.

\cgesubsec{Groups}

A blank \cmd{group} keeps the account. Accounts with the same group are merged
by \manref{cge_aggregate}, and must have the same role. After aggregation,
\cmd{cge_map using} the same template maps each group with its members' role,
and pairs it with the group of its members' pairs.

\cgesec{Methods and formulas}

With $x_{ij}$ the cell in row $i$ and column $j$ of the SAM, the totals of
account $k$ are
\begin{equation*}
\text{\cmd{row_total}}_k = \sum_j x_{kj}, \qquad
\text{\cmd{column_total}}_k = \sum_i x_{ik}, \qquad
\text{\cmd{difference}}_k = \text{\cmd{row_total}}_k - \text{\cmd{column_total}}_k .
\end{equation*}
When \cmd{cge_map using} pairs an activity $a$ whose pair is blank, it chooses
the commodity
\begin{equation*}
c(a) = \operatorname*{arg\,max}_{c \in C} x_{ac},
\end{equation*}
the largest payment of a commodity's column to the activity's row; a tie, or
no positive payment, is an error. The \cmd{commodities()} list then starts with
the paired commodities, in the order of the activities, followed by the other
commodities in the order of the SAM. Every other option lists its accounts in
the order of the SAM, except that for \cmd{territorial()} the commodity that
households consume is listed before its activity. \Chapref{chap:samtools}
describes the template in \secref{sec:template}.
