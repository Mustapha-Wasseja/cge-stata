\cgeentry{cge_region}{Link single-country models through bilateral trade}

\cgesec{Description}

\cmd{cge_region} (from version 0.12.0) links calibrated single-country models
through bilateral trade, as multi-region models such as GTAP do
\citep{hertel1997}. Each region is a model of its own, from its own SAM, with
its own closure and shocks. \cmd{cge_region add} adds the model in memory, or
one saved with \manref{cge_save}, as a region; \cmd{cge_region select} brings a
region's model into memory, where \manref{cge_shock}, \manref{cge_closure},
\manref{cge_results} and the other commands act on it. \cmd{cge_region trade}
reads the base trade between the regions, route by route, and
\cmd{cge_region solve} solves the regions together: for each commodity a
region trades with the others, its world import price, average tariff and
world export price are set by the links, with imports a CES aggregate of the
routes into it at prices that include each route's tariff. Each region's
solution is stored as a simulation in its own model.

\cgesec{Quick start}

Two calibrated models as regions, a unit of the second's SAM worth two world
units
\begin{stcode}
cge_region add A
cge_region add B using modelB.cgem, units(2)
\end{stcode}
The base trade between them, from data in memory; the tariffs between them
removed; the regions solved together
\begin{stcode}
cge_region trade, exporter(exp) importer(imp) commodity(com) value(v) tariff(t)
cge_region tariff, from(A B) to(A B) value(0)
cge_region solve, name(fta)
\end{stcode}
The routes of the solution as data; region A in memory, to report on it
\begin{stcode}
cge_region results, sim(fta) clear
cge_region select A
\end{stcode}

\cgesec{Options}

\begin{optdesc}
\item[\optarg{un}{its}{\#}] is the value in world units of a unit of the
region's SAM, so that regions with SAMs in different currencies or scales
share one world unit; route values are in world units.

\item[\optarg{exp}{orter}{varname} \optarg{imp}{orter}{varname}
\optarg{com}{modity}{varname}] name string variables with the regions and the
commodity of each route; each commodity must be in both regions, exported by
the one and imported by the other. \optarg{val}{ue}{varname} names the base
value of the route at world prices, in world units: the routes into a region
may not exceed its imports of the commodity, nor those out of it its exports.
\optarg{tar}{iff}{varname} gives the base tariff of a route; where it is
missing, the importer's average. The rest of the world takes the difference
between each region's trade of a linked commodity and its routes with the
other regions, with the tariff that gives the importer's average.

\item[\optarg{sig}{ma}{spec} \optarg{row}{elasticity}{spec}] give the elasticity
of substitution among the sources of imports, and the elasticity of the rest
of the world's demand for a region's exports: one number, or
\argu{commodity}\cmd{:}\argu{\#} pairs.

\item[\optarg{}{from}{regions} \optarg{}{to}{regions}
\optarg{com}{modities}{list}] of \cmd{cge_region tariff} choose the routes,
by exporter (\cmd{rest} for the rest of the world), importer and commodity;
\optarg{val}{ue}{\#} sets their tariffs and \optarg{sc}{ale}{\#} multiplies
them. They stay until changed or reset.

\item[\optarg{imp}{ort}{\#} \optarg{exp}{ort}{\#}] of \cmd{cge_region world}
set the rest of the world's export price of the commodities, and the price
that shifts its demand for the regions' exports, relative to the base;
\opt{}{reset} puts them and every route's tariff back at the base.

\item[\optarg{}{name}{name}] names the simulation that \cmd{cge_region solve}
stores in every region. \optarg{}{start}{alone} starts Newton's method from
each region solved on its own at the base links.
\end{optdesc}

\cgesec{Remarks and examples}

In a region, the world prices and tariffs of the commodities it trades with
the other regions are set by the links: they are changed with
\cmd{cge_region tariff} and \cmd{cge_region world}, and a shock to them with
\manref{cge_shock} is an error. Every other shock of a region, its closure and
its other commodities are its own. The regions and their trade last for the
session: each region is saved with \manref{cge_save} after
\cmd{cge_region select}, and the routes as data, to add them again. Two
regions, the synthetic SAM and its

\cgesec{Stored results}

\cmd{cge_region solve} stores the following in \cmd{r()}:
\begin{storedres}
\resgroup{Scalars}
\res{r(iterations)} & Newton iterations\\
\res{r(N_regions)} & regions\\
\res{r(N_links)} & link variables\\
\resgroup{Macros}
\res{r(name)} & name of the simulation\\
\end{storedres}
\cmd{cge_region list} stores \cmd{r(regions)}, \cmd{r(current)},
\cmd{r(N_regions)} and \cmd{r(N_routes)}; \cmd{cge_region results} stores
\cmd{r(sim)} and \cmd{r(N_routes)}.
