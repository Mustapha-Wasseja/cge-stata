. cge_modlib list

The model library of cge-stata
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model      Kind    Title
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
exchange   system  An exchange economy of two goods and two consumers
crusoe     nlp     Robinson Crusoe: consumption and leisure, a planner's nonlinear programme
ramsey     nlp     A Ramsey growth model: optimal saving over 30 years, a nonlinear programme
markets    system  Two markets solved block by block with named models
hs071      nlp     Hock and Schittkowski's problem 71, a nonlinear programme with bounds
transport  nlp     Dantzig's transportation problem: least-cost shipments, a linear programme
ras        nlp     Balancing a matrix by cross entropy, whose solution is the RAS balance
knapsack   minlp   A knapsack of eight items: binary choices, a mixed-integer programme
cournot    nash    Cournot duopoly and a Stackelberg leader, Nash equilibria
mpsge      system  Two goods and a tax on labour, written in the form of MPSGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
cge_modlib describe model shows a model; cge_modlib run model runs it;
cge_modlib copy model copies it to the current folder
